Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

1. Overview

Kleine is a privately operated AI assistant powered by OpenClaw, intended exclusively for authorized personal use. It is not a public service.

This policy concerns information handled by the assistant. This informational website does not connect to Google accounts, collect OAuth credentials, or store access or refresh tokens.

2. Google account access

Kleine accesses Google services through gog (Google Workspace CLI) only after a user explicitly authorizes access using OAuth 2.0. Depending on the permissions granted, it may access Gmail messages, email metadata, calendar events, and other authorized Google account information. Access is limited to the OAuth permissions granted by the user.

3. How information is used

Google account information is used solely to fulfill requests made by authorized users. Examples include reading or organizing email, drafting or sending messages, and managing calendar events, where the granted permissions allow these actions.

4. Data storage and security

OAuth credentials and operational data, such as request history and information needed to carry out tasks, may be stored within the assistant’s configured environment. This draft does not assert encryption, security certifications, or a fixed retention period.

Owner verification required: confirm what is stored, who can access it, the safeguards in place, and how logs, backups, and deletion are handled. Verify that those safeguards meet the applicable Google API requirements.

5. AI processing

Information may be processed by configured AI model providers when necessary to fulfill authorized requests. It does not necessarily remain local to the assistant’s environment.

Owner verification required: identify the providers actually used and disclose their relevant processing, retention, and human-access practices. Confirm whether provider terms and settings prevent Google user data from being used to train or improve generalized AI models; do not assume that they do. Do not send Google user data to a provider unless the applicable Google policies and user consent permit the transfer and processing.

6. Data sharing

Kleine does not sell personal information or use Google user data for advertising. Data may be transmitted to configured service providers, including AI model providers, when required to fulfill authorized requests.

Owner verification required: confirm that actual transfers, provider agreements, and user consent match these disclosures and the applicable Limited Use requirements.

7. Retention and revocation

Users can revoke Kleine’s Google OAuth access in their Google Account connections settings. Revocation prevents further access using that authorization; it does not automatically delete previously processed information, stored task history, backups, or data retained by service providers.

Owner verification required: document actual retention and deletion practices, including how users can request deletion, before approving this policy. No automatic deletion or specific retention period is promised by this draft.

8. Google API Services User Data Policy

Kleine’s use of Google API data must adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements where applicable. These requirements limit data use to permitted user-facing features, restrict transfers and human access, and prohibit advertising and other disallowed uses of covered data, including derived data.

This is a statement of applicable obligations, not a claim of established compliance, Google verification, or approval. The owner must verify the assistant’s actual behavior and provider arrangements against these requirements before publishing a final compliance statement.

9. This website

These static pages use no analytics, tracking scripts, cookies, third-party fonts, or external runtime dependencies. The hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process technical request information to serve the site under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Hosting-related processing is separate from the assistant’s Google account access.

10. Contact

Authorized users can contact Kleine’s operator through their existing communication channel for privacy questions or deletion requests.
